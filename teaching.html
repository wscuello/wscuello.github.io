Teaching

Teaching mathematics through structure, intuition, and application

I am privileged and honored to be a tenure-track professor at Greenfield Community College.

My main goal is to disseminate mathematics into digestible forms for students from all socioeconomic backgrounds and many career paths. I also serve as an advisor for the STEM Club, where we tackle competitive math problems through AMATYC and explore recreational mathematics and logic puzzles. I love taking real-world scenarios and finding mathematical ways to approximate their behavior, and I hope to share this enthusiasm with every student who walks through my office or classroom door.
